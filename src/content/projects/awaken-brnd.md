## The client

**Awaken Brand** is a Spanish sportswear brand that makes high-performance clothing and accessories for training, for both men and women. The brand needed an online store that reflects its look and that the team can run day to day.

I've been their developer since the brand first went online, and I own everything technical.

## From a WordPress shop to Shopify

### 2023: the first store

I registered the **awakenbrnd.store** domain, set up the DNS and hosting, and built the first shop on **WordPress with WooCommerce**. I used the Divi builder for the pages and MailPoet for the newsletter. It gave the brand a working Spanish-language store with product categories, legal pages and email sign-ups from day one.

### 2025: the move to Shopify

A self-hosted WordPress shop needs constant care: hosting, plugin and security updates, and a checkout to look after. For a small brand, a hosted e-commerce platform made more sense. In autumn 2025 I migrated the store to **Shopify**:

- **Catalogue and content:** I moved the products, collections and pages over to Shopify.
- **Old links:** URLs from the old store's pages redirect to their new Shopify addresses, such as the returns policy.
- **Domain:** it stayed at the same registrar, with its DNS pointed at Shopify, so the store's address never changed.
- **Payments and checkout:** these are now handled by Shopify, including Shop Pay.

## The custom theme

The new storefront is a **custom theme built on Horizon**, Shopify's own theme. Starting from a well-supported base means the store keeps getting Shopify's platform improvements, while the theme carries the brand:

- **Bilingual storefront:** Spanish and English, with a language selector.
- **Merchandising for the catalogue:** collections for men, women and accessories, plus "Just dropped" and "Last chance". There are filters by availability, price, gender, size and colour.
- **Product pages:**
  - colour swatches
  - size guides
  - customer reviews
  - Shop Pay checkout
- **Marketing:**
  - a free-shipping announcement bar
  - a Klaviyo newsletter sign-up ("Join our community")
  - a brand video and a "Join the Awaken fam" community section

## What I learned

- **Pick the platform for the client, not for the developer.** A custom WordPress build gave me full control, but the brand needed something they could run day to day without me. Moving to Shopify shifted my work from keeping the store running to improving it.
- **A migration is mostly about details.** URLs, redirects, translations and the domain have to move without breaking links or search rankings.

## Status

The store is live and I keep looking after it: theme updates, new collections and technical support. It currently sells within Spain, so product listings appear only to visitors browsing from there.
